About Sharad Institute of Technology

College History

Established in 1983, Sharad Institute of Technology (SITCOE) has grown to become one of the premier engineering colleges in Maharashtra. Over the decades, SITCOE has been dedicated to providing quality education, fostering innovation, and shaping future engineers with a strong ethical foundation.

Alumni

Our alumni network is spread across the globe, excelling in diverse fields such as software development, research, academia, and entrepreneurship. SITCOE alumni continue to contribute significantly to industry and society, maintaining a strong bond with their alma mater.

College Campus

The SITCOE campus is situated in a serene environment, equipped with modern facilities including spacious classrooms, advanced laboratories, a well-stocked library, sports complex, and comfortable hostels. The campus is designed to encourage learning, creativity, and collaboration among students.

About Departments

SITCOE offers undergraduate and postgraduate courses in various engineering disciplines:

  • Computer Science and Engineering
  • Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering
  • Mechanical Engineering
  • Civil Engineering
  • Information Technology

Each department is staffed by experienced faculty members and equipped with state-of-the-art laboratories to provide hands-on experience.
