Admissions at Sharad Institute of Technology College of Engineering

Eligibility Criteria

To enroll in SITCOE, prospective students must meet the following eligibility requirements:

  • Completion of 10+2 or equivalent examination with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.
  • A minimum aggregate score of 50% (45% for reserved categories) in the qualifying examination.
  • Valid score in the Maharashtra Common Entrance Test (MHT-CET) or JEE Main examination.
  • For postgraduate courses, candidates must hold a relevant undergraduate degree with a minimum percentage as prescribed.

Application Process

The admission process at SITCOE is designed to be straightforward and transparent:

  • Online Application: Candidates must fill out the online application form available on the official SITCOE website.
  • Document Submission: Upload necessary documents including mark sheets, identity proof, caste certificates (if applicable), and photographs.
  • Entrance Exam Scores: Submit valid MHT-CET or JEE Main scores during application.
  • Merit List & Counseling: Based on entrance exam ranks, merit lists will be published followed by a counseling session for seat allocation.
  • Fee Payment: Selected candidates must pay the admission fee online or at the campus to confirm their seat.

Fee Structure

SITCOE offers competitive fee structures for various programs:

  • Undergraduate Programs: ₹1,20,000 per annum (includes tuition, labs, and library fees)
  • Postgraduate Programs: ₹1,50,000 per annum
  • Additional fees may apply for hostel accommodation, transport, and extracurricular activities.
  • Fee concessions and installment options are available for eligible candidates.

Scholarships & Financial Aid

SITCOE is committed to supporting meritorious and deserving students through various scholarships:

  • Merit-Based Scholarships: Awarded to top-performing students based on entrance exam ranks and academic records.
  • Category-Based Scholarships: Available for SC, ST, OBC, and other reserved categories as per government policies.
  • Need-Based Financial Aid: Provided to students from economically weaker sections after evaluation.
  • Students are encouraged to apply early and submit required documents to avail scholarships.
