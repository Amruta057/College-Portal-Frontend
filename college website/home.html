Welcome to Sharad Institute of Technology College of Engineering

About Our College

Sharad Institute of Technology College of Engineering (SITCOE) is one of Maharashtra's premier engineering institutes. With a commitment to excellence in education, innovation, and character-building, SITCOE prepares students to become future-ready professionals. Our mission is to develop competent engineers with a strong foundation in technical and interpersonal skills.

Departments Overview

Computer Science

Focused on software development, AI, and data science with cutting-edge labs and industry tie-ups.

Mechanical Engineering

Equips students with skills in manufacturing, thermodynamics, design, and CAD/CAM technologies.

Civil Engineering

Expertise in structural design, surveying, environment, and smart infrastructure development.

Electronics & Telecommunication

Blending analog/digital systems, IoT, and embedded systems with real-world applications.

Artificial Intelligence & Data Science

Modern curriculum with machine learning, big data, and neural networks for future-ready engineers.
